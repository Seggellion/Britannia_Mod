## Placeholder generator boundary

`tools/generate_flower_placeholders.py` and `tools/flower_placeholder_hashes.json` remain historical safety tooling for the original generated asset set. The 98 in-world flower textures and this manifest are now owner-directed integration outputs and are no longer byte-owned by that ledger. The generator's guarded overwrite must refuse these replacements; it must not be used to regenerate them.

Other generated item models, item textures, tags, and shared resources remain covered by their existing ledger entries. The 16 transparent in-world mask files above are intentionally retained runtime placeholders, but their selection is now governed by the approved Illustrator mask matrix rather than the generator's former stage-number rule.

## Replacement contract

Replacement art must preserve the paired 128 x 128 canvas, full-canvas UV layout, transparent padding, and base/mask pixel alignment unless both rendering passes are intentionally revised together. Base alpha must be zero wherever mask alpha is nonzero. Visible authored-mask RGB must be exactly white. Model JSON paths and the two-texture canonical model graph remain unchanged.
