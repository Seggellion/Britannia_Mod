# Starfarer’s Medallion Phase 2 Context

Authoritative scope lock for the Patch-18 wearable 3D asset project.

- PATCH 18 ONLY. Do not merge, transplant, or depend on Patch 19.
- Equipment slot: `EquipmentSlot.CHEST`.
- Wear permission: owner only, enforced in common/server-authoritative code for both right-click equip and armor-slot insertion.
- Item architecture: `StarfarersMedallionItem extends Item implements Equipable`; do not use `ArmorItem`.
- Renderer: vanilla client-only torso render layer anchored to `PlayerModel.body`; do not use GeckoLib for this item.
- Asset architecture: one shared vanilla 3D model for item and worn rendering.
- Texture: one production 64x64 RGBA PNG.
- Editable source: commit `starfarers_medallion.bbmodel` with the exported vanilla `elements` JSON and PNG.
- Lore: Treatment 2 (ceremonial):
  - `Starfarer’s Medallion`
  - `Bestowed upon travelers who answered a call from beyond the skies of Britannia.`
  - `Struck for those who answered the call.`
  - `Squadron 42 - Manchester, 2956`
- Blessed diagnostic fix: approved; diagnostic visibility may include `inventory.armor`, but item location never authorizes delivery or restoration.
- Equip sound: existing vanilla generic equip sound (`SoundEvents.ARMOR_EQUIP_GENERIC`); no new sound asset.
- Wearing the medallion occupies CHEST and therefore blocks a chestplate or elytra; the owner explicitly accepts this.
- Do not add Curios, Accessories, custom equipment state, a new inventory slot, custom networking, or gameplay bonuses.
