# UltimaCraft Splash Research

## Purpose and method

Milestone 4 replaces the normal Minecraft 1.21.1 splash list with original short lines grounded in Ultima, Ultima Online, Shroud of the Avatar, and mechanics already implemented by UltimaCraft. Research preceded drafting. Official Ultima Online and Shroud of the Avatar material was used for world and lore facts; repository source was used for project-specific claims. Factual notes below are paraphrased in original wording and are not copied promotional or narrative passages.

The shipped list governs normal random title-screen rotation. Minecraft's hardcoded December 24, January 1, October 31, and rare username greetings are renderer behavior rather than resource entries and remain intentionally unchanged.

## Research boundary

Lines are newly written microcopy. Named nouns and compact commands are used as referential vocabulary; no official prose, quest dialogue, song lyric, or long quotation is reproduced. Where a line is deliberately whimsical, it avoids presenting the joke as a mechanical or historical fact. Precise UltimaCraft claims are limited to behavior visible in repository source.
