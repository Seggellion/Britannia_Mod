# Custom Block Inventory

> Read-only source and resource audit performed against the repository working tree on 2026-09-14. Namespace: `britannia_mod`.

## Executive Summary

The mod registers **559 unique custom blocks**. The category totals reconcile exactly: **146 architectural**, **106 environmental**, **195 decorative model**, **98 functional or interactive**, **14 technical or internal**, and **0 unclear/owner-review**. The dedicated decorative catalogue therefore contains **195 blocks**.

Using the counting rule below, **503 blocks use specialized/custom presentation**. **237 blocks use a block entity and/or custom block renderer** (the renderer set is a subset of the block-entity set). There are **0 low-confidence classifications** and **0 classifications requiring owner review**.

Discovery traced the constructor wiring in `BritanniaMod`, every live `DeferredRegister<Block>`, the main registry's literal declarations and helper/loop families, the 57-value store-sign enum loop, the 77-ID fish loop, the painting loop, banner/large-structure/Grabby feature registries, block-entity registrations, client renderer registrations, block items, creative-tab references, tests, blockstates, recursive model parents, textures, translations, loot tables, tags, recipes, and world/resource-management references. Registration IDs were deduplicated and totals were calculated from the master rows below.

**Specialized/custom presentation rule.** A row counts when its effective presentation is a custom-element or Blockbench-style JSON model, multipart blockstate, generated architectural state/model family, plant cross, animated GeckoLib model, custom block-entity/dynamic renderer, or flat/wall object model. Standard cubes, plain vanilla-parent models, invisible/internal blocks, and unresolved missing-blockstate rows do not count. This is a rendering inventory, not a claim that every non-cube is decorative.

**Limitations.** This is static analysis: no client/server was launched because the task forbids generated/build changes. Runtime datapacks, server-provided structures, and world NBT can alter reachability but not compiled registrations. A missing conventional asset is an audit finding, not automatically a defect: internal blocks may intentionally render invisibly, some families render through block entities, and many blocks use custom/no drops. Vanilla model/texture references are treated as external and valid.

## Architectural and Environmental Separation

- **Architectural trim remains architectural.** `stone_finial`, `dark_stone_finial`, `dark_stone_buttress`, `sandstone_battlement`, `sandstone_column`, plaster supports, ceiling panels/joists and modeled archways are object-like geometry but repeatable parts of a building envelope.
- **Roofs remain architectural even when adaptive or modeled.** Eight flat roof registrations carry `AdaptiveRoofBlockEntity` state and custom variants. The four `*_roof_base` registrations have no blockstate and only supply base states to stair registrations, so they are technical/internal.
- **Fences, gates, doors, windows and ladders remain architectural.** Multiblock footprints, custom collision, automatic closing or locks are secondary. `window_collision` alone is technical because it is an invisible collision helper.
- **Lighting is decorative.** Sconces, lamps, chandeliers, torches, candelabra and braziers emit light, but their identity is a recognizable placed fixture; light/nudge behavior is secondary.
- **Farm plot, trellis and scarecrow split by role.** `house_farm_plot`, `trellis_block`, soils, crops and managed flowers participate in planting/growth/harvest and are environmental. `scarecrow` has farm-only Adventure placement but no crop mechanic, so it is decorative.
- **Natural modeled objects remain environmental when they are world material.** Ores, managed deposits, rocks, cave/flowstone formations, vegetation and fruit-tree cells are environmental even with elaborate geometry. Fractional dirt/grass is terrain; fractional deepslate-cobblestone is construction.
- **Fish blocks are decorative presentations of resources.** Fish items are caught resources, but `WeightedFishBlock` exists to deliberately place a catch on tagged ground, preserve its weight/type in a block entity and pick it back up in Adventure mode. The block form is display, not aquatic ecology/worldgen.
- **Display case remains decorative; inventory containers do not.** `display_case` holds one rendered exhibit and its connection geometry is presentation-driven. Chests, armoires and crates expose meaningful inventories/menus and are functional.
- **Curtain cells and cemetery flora are props.** Curtain top/foundation/bottom and arranged grave flowers are authored scenery, not construction or unmanaged vegetation.
- **Medium-confidence boundaries.** `cave`, `cave_floor`, `pool_of_blood`, terrain fractions and `dungeon_moongate_top` have implementation evidence for the selected category but no explicit owner taxonomy. None is too ambiguous to inventory, so owner-review is empty.
